# Outreach emails

Send these from your own email address, to special education teachers, case managers and special education coordinators whose addresses are publicly listed on school or district staff pages. Personalize the first line for each person. Send small batches (15–25 a day), and stop emailing anyone who asks.

Ask people to try the **built-in fictional sample** first. That way nobody has to put a real IEP into a new tool before they've checked with their district.

---

## 1. Special education teacher or case manager

**Subject:** Free tool for IEP-at-a-glance sheets

Hi Ms. ___,

I saw you're a special education teacher at ___. I built a free tool that turns an IEP PDF into one-page sheets for the classroom teacher, the para and the sub, with accommodations, the behavior plan, health alerts and goals in plain language. Every line shows the IEP page it came from, and you check and edit everything before printing.

It runs entirely in the browser. Nothing is uploaded or saved, there's no account, nothing goes to an AI service, and it works with Wi-Fi turned off.

You can try it in two minutes with a built-in fictional IEP: [link]

Would it save you time when you share IEPs with your team? Any feedback helps.

Thanks,
[Name]

## 2. Special education coordinator or director

**Subject:** Free, offline IEP-at-a-glance tool for your case managers

Hi Dr. ___,

Case managers spend real time turning IEPs into summaries for gen ed teachers and paras, and often those summaries leave out the behavior plan or goals. I built a free, open-source tool that drafts those one-page sheets from the IEP PDF, with page references so staff can check them.

It's designed to be easy to approve: it's a single web page that blocks all network requests, stores nothing, sends nothing to any AI service (its small sorting model is built into the page), and can run as an offline file your district hosts itself. A one-page summary for your technology team is attached.

Demo with fictional IEPs: [link]

Would a 15-minute call to see if it fits your process be useful?

[Name]

## Follow-up (one, after a week)

**Subject:** Re: Free tool for IEP-at-a-glance sheets

Hi ___, just following up once. If it's useful, the fictional sample shows the whole flow in about two minutes: [link]. If not, no worries, and I won't email again.

## What to track

- Replies, and who tried the sample
- Who used it with a real IEP (ask, since the tool can't tell you)
- Who used it again for a second student
- Which IEP formats it handled poorly (ask for the heading names, never the IEP itself)
