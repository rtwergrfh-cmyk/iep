Individualized Education Program for Jules Okafor

Date of birth: 01/09/2015. Grade 5. Parent: Ngozi Okafor. IEP meeting date: 10/02/2026.

About Jules

Jules is a curious, funny fifth grader who loves building things and drawing comics. He works well with his friend Jayden, and he tends to get off task when seated near Marcus during group work. His little sister, Rosie, attends the same school. Jules reads at a second-grade level and decodes slowly; his listening comprehension is at grade level.

What the team agreed to

Teachers will read test questions and directions aloud to Jules. Jules gets double time (2x) on state tests and time and a half (1.5x) on classwork. Never ask him to read aloud in front of the class. He may give answers orally instead of in writing when the skill being checked is not writing.

Keeping Jules safe

Jules has a seizure disorder. If he has a seizure that lasts more than 3 minutes, call 911 and then the nurse. He takes levetiracetam at home every morning and evening, and the nurse keeps his rescue medication (a nasal spray) in the health office.

Support he will receive

Jules will receive specialized reading instruction for 30 minutes daily in the resource room with Mr. Daniels, his reading interventionist. The speech-language pathologist will consult with his teacher 30 minutes a month.

What we are working toward

By October 2027, Jules will read a grade 3 passage at 90 words correct per minute, as measured by monthly fluency checks.
